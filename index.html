Duckweed Population Growth Simulation

Virtual version of the six-beaker duckweed population-growth experiment

Your task

Imagine that you have set up six 100 mL beakers, each starting with 5 healthy duckweed plants. Beakers 1–3 have normal CO₂ conditions. Beakers 4–6 have high CO₂ conditions.

Advance the simulation one week at a time. At each week, record the number of plants and number of leaves in every beaker. Use these data to complete your worksheet.

The simulation includes small differences between replicate beakers, just as a real experiment would. The underlying populations are fixed, so repeating the simulation gives the same dataset.
